What the numbers mean

A human body runs about 3.7 × 1013 cells in some 70 litres, which is 528,571 cells per cubic millimetre. So the density of any one aspect in a body is simply the per-cell count multiplied by that.

Aether is not chosen

Every atom sits at its own seat on the compass — the same arrangement the kardia holds, carried down into every cell of the body. The figure above is drawn from the compass’s own coordinates, viewed from above; near seats are brighter than far ones. Note the compass is an armillary in three dimensions, so any flat view of it foreshortens — this angle is simply the one that separates all sixteen seats without any two merging.

Aether is never acquired and never counted separately. The Aether in a cell equals the count of that cell’s highest pair — an Apprentice has one of each aspect and one Aether, a Master has a hundred and a hundred. It follows the maximum rather than being taken in.

Ambient Aether behaves the same way: a region’s Aether reading is roughly the maximum of its aspect densities. Ground reading Air 20, Earth 24 also reads Aether ≈ 24.

So a cell’s whole contents come to (aspects × count) + count. A Grandmaster holding four pairs carries 9,000 atoms in every cell: a thousand of each of eight aspects, and a thousand Aether.

The bonded charge cannot be spent

None of this is fuel. Bonded mana is fixed in place and cannot be used for a working — it is structure. What it buys is lifespan, physical attributes, and speed of flow. The mana a person actually casts with is held loose in the kardia and spent.
