The Aspect Cycle

A fantasy novel and the world beneath it. The magic system is derived rather than declared: the relationships between the nineteen aspects follow from one geometric law, and everything downstream — atomic structure, anatomy, the writing system, the spell notation — falls out of that.

These instruments were built to check that the derivations hold. They are working tools, not illustrations; every figure below is computed, and none of it was assigned by hand.
